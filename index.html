Who's behind the lens

Meet Donna Nugent

I'm Donna, a photographer, people person and natural light lover with a soft spot for colour, genuine connection and the little details that bring a story to life.

With over 20 years behind the camera, I've learned that great photography is about more than how something looks. It's about how people feel, the personality behind a brand and capturing those moments that make a story worth sharing.

My work spans lifestyle portraits, branding, food, products and commercial photography. I love working with people who have a passion for what they do and want their photographs to reflect it. Whether it's showcasing a business, bringing a product to life or capturing the people behind a brand, I'm all about creating images that feel natural, vibrant and full of personality.

My background includes photographing over 200 weddings, where I learned to read a room, think on my feet and help people feel at ease. These days, that same approach carries into every shoot. I take the time to get to know you, understand your vision and create a relaxed space where you can be yourself. And if being in front of the camera feels a little uncomfortable, you're in good hands!

Originally from country Victoria, I've spent years exploring, learning and photographing people and businesses across different places. These days, I split my time between Torquay on Victoria's Surf Coast and the Byron Bay region of NSW, working with clients across both coastlines.

I love collaborating with dreamers, doers and independent businesses who care about what they put into the world. If you have a story to tell, a business to share or an idea you're ready to bring to life, I'd love to hear about it.

A photographer's style is simply how they see the world. Mine is warm light, honest colour and people who are actually enjoying themselves.

The journey so far

Three decades, told in chapters

A little glimpse into the path that brought Donna here, one chapter at a time.

Country Victoria

A camera before a plan

Growing up in country Victoria, Donna's passion for photography began at just 14. Her favourite subjects were the horses on the family property, often turning her friends into models and developing her prints in a dusty darkroom inside a horse stable.

The travel years

Chasing the light

Before settling into her career, Donna set off to explore the world. She loved people-watching in different places, noticing how natural light danced across faces and how quickly someone could relax once they felt truly seen.

Melbourne

Learning the craft

Donna moved to Melbourne to study photography, working in a photographic printing lab while assisting talented professional photographers and connecting with other creatives and mentors. It was a time of learning, experimenting and developing her skills.

The wedding years

200+ weddings, countless connections

Donna photographed over 200 weddings, refining her skills with the camera and people. She learned to put people at ease, read a room and capture the love, laughter and energy of every celebration.

The Surf Coast decade

From weddings to brand storytelling

A decade ago, Donna put down roots on Victoria's Surf Coast and turned that same warmth and curiosity towards brand portraits, food, products and commercial photography. She worked with businesses across the Surf Coast, Geelong and Melbourne, helping bring their people and stories to life.

Now

Byron Bay and beyond

Today, Donna works between Torquay, Victoria, and the Byron Bay region of NSW, bringing her relaxed approach, love of natural light and eye for genuine moments to businesses across both coastlines.

Client stories

What clients actually walk away with

Not just pretty pictures. Here is the kind of transformation a session with Donna typically delivers.

Branding portraits

From dreading the shoot to using the photos everywhere

The challenge

A business owner who had avoided being on camera for years needed headshots for a new website and LinkedIn.

The approach

Donna spent the first twenty minutes just talking, no camera in sight, then shot handheld while they worked, never posed stiffly against a plain wall.

The result

A full image set now used across the website, LinkedIn and socials, with the client saying the photos finally look like them.

Commercial & documentary

Real people, real workplace, no stock-photo feel

The challenge

A business needed imagery of staff and spaces for reports and web use, wary of anything that looked staged.

The approach

Donna shot documentary style across a working day, staying in the background so people forgot the camera was there.

The result

A library of authentic images the business still pulls from for reports, recruitment and its website years later.

Food & product

Turning a menu into something people want to eat

The challenge

A cafe needed food photography that felt vibrant and appetite driving, not flat or clinical.

The approach

Donna shot on location in natural light, styling each dish simply so the real colour and texture led the frame.

The result

Images used across the menu, packaging and social feed that gave the brand a noticeably more premium feel.

Before you book

Questions people ask before trusting someone with their photos

I genuinely hate having my photo taken. Is that a problem?+
Honestly, that describes most of Donna's clients. Years of photographing 200+ weddings taught her how to get a total stranger to relax within minutes, no forced posing, no awkward silences. If you can show up and be willing to have a bit of a laugh, she will handle the rest.
How experienced are you, really?+
Over 20 years behind the camera, spanning wedding photography, branding portraits, commercial and documentary work, food and product photography, for individuals and for businesses like Ocean Rd Consulting, Conexus Financial and QCE.
What happens to my photos after the shoot?+
You get a private online gallery to view, download and share your high resolution images from any device, no chasing files over email. See the client gallery section below.
Do you only shoot in Byron Bay?+
No. Donna is based between the Byron Bay region, NSW and the Surf Coast, VIC, and regularly shoots across Northern NSW, Tweed Heads, the Gold Coast and Victoria.
Will the photos actually look like a "photoshoot"?+
The goal is the opposite. Donna's whole approach is built around natural light, real expressions and minimal posing, so the end result looks like an honest, higher quality version of you, not a stiff studio setup.
